A page with purpose

Made for the
practice of writing.

Genkō yōshi—literally “manuscript paper”—has long given Japanese writing a measured rhythm. Each square holds one character, making spacing and composition visible.

This digital version preserves that structure while adding the tools a modern learner needs. No clutter. No streaks to maintain. Just a considered space to return to your writing.

Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and start practicing immediately. Your sheets are stored locally in your current browser.

Can I use it on a tablet or phone?

Yes. The workspace is responsive and installable from supported browsers. A tablet and stylus offer the most natural writing experience.

Where is my practice saved?

Your sheets stay in your browser’s local storage. Clearing site data removes them, so export important work as a PDF backup.

Can I print my sheets?

Yes. Use Export PDF, then save or print through your device’s standard print dialog.
